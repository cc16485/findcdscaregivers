Why it matters

The question most agencies cannot answer

Nearly every Consumer Directed Services provider in Missouri assumes you already have someone. Their forms ask who your caregiver will be. If your answer is "I don't have anybody," you are usually told to go and find one yourself.

That is the gap this service exists to close. It is included at no cost when you enroll in CDS with Caring Companions, and there is no cost to take part in CDS either. Medicaid funds the care.

Questions

Frequently asked

Can I browse the caregivers myself?
No, and that is deliberate. We do not publish anyone's name, photo or contact details. People looking for caregiving work trust us with their information, and many of them are women working alone in other people's homes. We match on your county and your hours, then make the introduction.
What does it cost?
Nothing. The matching is included when you enroll in CDS with Caring Companions, and the CDS program itself is free to Medicaid participants. Medicaid funds the care.
Do I have to take whoever you suggest?
Not at all. In this program you are the employer and you choose who works for you. Say no as many times as you need to and we will keep looking.
Are they checked?
Yes. Before we introduce anyone they go through the Missouri Family Care Safety Registry and we check the Employee Disqualification List. We only introduce people who are cleared.
What if there is nobody in my county yet?
We will tell you honestly rather than leave you waiting. We are building the network across all 27 counties, and we recruit specifically for the counties where families are waiting.
I already have someone in mind. Do I need this?
No. If you already have a family member or friend who will provide the care, go straight to Caring Companions and enroll. This service is for people who do not have anyone.
