For families

Tell us what you need and we will find someone

Two minutes, and a real person calls you back. There is no cost, and nothing here commits you to anything. If you would rather just talk it through, call 417-218-2888.

We never ask for a Social Security number on this form, and you should never send one by email or text. We do not publish anything you tell us.

What happens next

After you send this

  1. 1

    We call you

    Usually the same or next working day. We confirm Medicaid and talk through what you actually need.

  2. 2

    We look for someone in your county

    From people who have asked us for caregiving work and who we have already screened.

  3. 3

    You meet them and decide

    You are the employer in this program, so the choice is yours. Say no as often as you need to.
